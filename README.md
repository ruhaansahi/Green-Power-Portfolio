# Greenpower Racing — Team Portfolio

Documentation from the DAA "Leopards" Greenpower team's 2025 season, an
electric go-kart built and raced as part of the Greenpower Education Trust
competition. This isn't a software project; it's a written record of the
engineering, design, and sustainability work behind the kart.

## What's documented

### Design Aesthetics
CAD-driven design process for the kart's body panels and nose cone,
covering:
- Concept sketching through to 3D modelling in Fusion 360
- Aerodynamic simulation on nose cone shapes, and design constraints around
  a fixed 200mm foam nose cone and capped car dimensions
- Minimalist side panels and aero discs added specifically to cut wheel drag
- CAD models converted to orthographic drawings for laser cutting, with
  paper prototypes tested before committing to final manufacturing
- Lightweight aluminum chosen for side panels, balancing weight limits
  against sustainability goals

### Design Mechanics
The drivetrain and braking work, including:
- Gear ratio changes (driven gear reduced from 72 to 60 teeth) to raise top
  speed, with chain length recalculated to match
- Brake disc friction issues diagnosed and resolved through fine adjustment
  and repositioning the brake light sensor
- 3D-printed washers designed to align brake discs and cut friction
- Axle and wheel assembly problems worked through, including cases where
  assembly steps had to be reversed to reach hidden screws

### Sustainability
Concrete constraints the team held themselves to, not just stated goals:
- Fully digital workflow (iPad sketching instead of paper) ahead of 3D
  modelling
- No single-use plastic water bottles during the build
- Reused aluminum side panels and foam from the previous year's kart
- Group transport to the race to cut fuel use

### Sponsorship
The funding pitch process: a sponsor presentation, an email outreach
template, and a video campaign, leading to sponsorships from SCAFFCO
(body panel production) and the school's Parent Association.

## Team

12 students (grades 10–11) split across Design Aesthetics, Design
Mechanics, and Sponsorship sub-teams, with a faculty supervisor.
